# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Delivery

Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business days after an order leaves the warehouse. Express shipping takes 2 business days. Delivery estimates do not include weekends or public holidays.

## International Delivery

International shipping normally takes 7-12 business days. Customs inspections can extend that estimate, and customers are responsible for local import duties.

## Shipping Charges

Domestic orders of $75 or more qualify for free standard shipping. Express and international delivery charges are calculated during checkout and do not count toward the free-shipping threshold.
