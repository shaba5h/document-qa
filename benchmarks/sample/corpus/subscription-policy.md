# Subscription Policy

## Cancellation

A customer may cancel a subscription at any time. Access remains active until the end of the current billing period, and the subscription does not renew after that date.

## Refunds

An annual subscription is eligible for a full refund when cancellation is requested within 7 calendar days of the initial purchase. Monthly subscription payments are non-refundable.

## Renewal Notices

Acme sends an annual renewal notice 14 days before the renewal date. The notice is delivered to the account owner's email address.
