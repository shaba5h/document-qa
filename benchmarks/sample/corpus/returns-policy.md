# Product Returns and Exchanges

## Product Returns

Physical products purchased from Acme Store may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Returned items must be unused and kept in their original packaging. Return shipping charges are non-refundable unless Acme shipped the wrong item or the product arrived damaged.

## Exchanges

An exchange for a different size or color may be requested within 14 calendar days of delivery. Exchanges are available only while replacement stock remains available.

## Refund Processing

After a returned item passes inspection, Acme sends the approved refund to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. A customer's bank may need additional time to display the credit.
