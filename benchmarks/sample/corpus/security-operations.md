# Security Operations Standard

## Account Security

Employees must use multi-factor authentication for company systems. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must not be reused across personal and company accounts.

## Incident Response

The security team must acknowledge a critical security incident within 15 minutes. The incident commander then coordinates containment, investigation, and recovery.

## Backups

Encrypted production backups are retained for 30 days. Restore tests are performed once every quarter.
