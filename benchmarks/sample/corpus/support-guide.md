# Customer Support Guide

## Service Hours

The customer support team operates Monday through Friday from 09:00-18:00 UTC. Requests can be submitted through the support portal or by email.

## Response Targets

A critical request that blocks all customer access has an initial response target of 1 hour during service hours. A standard request has an initial response target of 2 business days.

Response targets describe when support begins investigating a request. They do not guarantee the time needed for a complete resolution.
