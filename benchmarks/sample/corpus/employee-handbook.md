# Employee Handbook

## Paid Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 paid vacation days per calendar year. They also receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Unused vacation days do not automatically carry into the next year.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely on Tuesday and Thursday when their role does not require an on-site presence. A manager must approve any additional remote-work day.

## Probation

New employees complete a probation period of 90 calendar days. Benefits and leave balances still accrue during probation.
